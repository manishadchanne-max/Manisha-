How results are read

  • Official result is the Remarks printed in the register (PASS / PROMOTED / FAIL). It is never recalculated.
  • Total and percentage are out of 600: English, Urdu, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics. Health & Physical Education and Environment Studies carry grades only and are not in the 600.
  • Class uses the percentage: 75 and above Distinction, 60 to 74.99 First Class, 35 to 59.99 Pass Class, below 35 Below threshold.
  • The register prints no percentage for PROMOTED students, so no class is shown for them.
  • Subject check flags any subject with a score under 35. A student can be PROMOTED despite this because of grace marks given by the college.
  • AB means absent in that component. It is shown as AB and never counted as zero in the detail view.
